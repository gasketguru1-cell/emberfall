# Flint, the lamplighter

Original character. Balanced all-rounder.

## Look

- Adult human, sturdy build, stylised proportions: head about one fifth of total height, large hands and boots.
- Long teal greatcoat with a flared hem, brass buttons, amber scarf.
- Wide-brimmed teal hat with an amber band, slightly battered.
- Clean-shaven, warm skin, calm expression.
- Carries a brass hand lantern with a lit flame. For rigging, the lantern is a separate prop held in the right hand.
- Dark trousers, heavy brown work boots, fingerless gloves.

## For generation

- Neutral A-pose, arms away from the body, legs apart, facing the camera.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no cast shadows, no text.
- Front view first; side and back views for multi-image generation.
- Stylised 3D game character, readable silhouette, clean shapes, no fine noise.

## In game

- Height 14 units, width 8 units (hurtbox). Scale the model to match.
- Faces +Z for Meshy rigging; the game turns it to face along the stage.
- Triangle budget: 30,000 or less. Textures: 2K or less.
