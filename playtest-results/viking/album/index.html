Fyrdsman Discovery Album

Odds are from 20 headless runs each, because the game has some randomness. Nothing found here is a zero-to-death on Flint whatever direction he drifts.

Tech chase

Looked like a zero-to-death on Potato Sack under every DI. It isn't: it works only if the Sack misses its tech.

#5

Missed-tech punish: Berserker dtilt, recall KOs Potato Sack at 0%

any DI, if the Sack misses its tech A+B on; axe stuck far right; dtilt w3 fh rc
  • The down-tilt knocks the Sack down; the recalled axe hits it on the floor and carries it off the far side
  • The combo counter resets on the knockdown, so this is not a true combo
  • Mashing out of hitstun stops the Sack from teching (each shield press restarts the 40-frame tech lockout)
  • Getting up in place still dies; any tech or roll away survives

Same route, the Sack techs the landing: it survives

tech in place dtilt w3 fh rc
  • Shield pressed in the 11 frames before landing
  • Tech in place, tech roll either way and a roll away from the floor all escape, on every DI
