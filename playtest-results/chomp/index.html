Chomp Chomp Discovery Album

Chomp Chomp tested in the real Emberfall engine: 51 search jobs, 6.49 million route branches and 274 million simulated frames, then every result replayed under DI, SDI, escape attempts, the level-9 CPU and late inputs. Every clip here was recorded from the game itself.

No game code, balance or mechanic was changed, and no glitch was fixed. Each zero-to-death is true only against the DI shown on its card; the level-9 CPU escapes all of them. The brief arrived cut off at "Explore thousands", read here as exploring thousands of routes.

Top discoveries, ranked

Ranked by how much each changes how Chomp is played, weighted toward what holds up under DI and human timing.

#1

Knell at 110%: coal, down throw, fsmash

no DI coalH grab_d fsmash
  • True against all 9 DI directions (34 of 270 defenders with SDI and escape attempts added)
  • Inputs 1 frame late: 30/30; 2 frames: 20/30
  • Also kills at 90, 100, 120 and 130%
  • Also works on Potato Sack
#2

Wall-swing pop

  • Swinging while pinned against the island's side wall keeps the swing running
  • Once he rises past the floor he snaps to where the swing would be: up to 49.9 units in one frame, onto the stage
  • 256 of 360 tested routes from below the ledge did it
  • Not fixed

Coal relay across the stage

coalH relay relay relay
  • Relay window: 3 frames either side of the mark; 1 frame off or less adds speed

#9

Ant anti-air with a full hop

  • Chomp full hops 20 frames after calling the ant: the rope swings up and the ant meets the jumping foe (8.8%)
  • Hopping at 26, 32 or 38 frames missed
  • The rope keeps the ant's progress, so moving Chomp swings the ant around the coal
#10

The rope is a ramp, not a hitbox

  • The rope never deals damage
  • A foe falling onto a sloped rope is slowed from 1.55 to about 0.3 per frame and slides down it toward the coal
  • A flat rope on the floor never blocks walking; jumping lifts the rope over a standing foe
#11

Ledge fsmash kills at 100%

  • Kills 4/4 at 100% against Flint and Potato Sack, and at 80% against Knell, with the level-9 CPU recovering
  • Hanging the coal off-stage with the ant hits the recovering CPU often (6–15%) but never stopped it recovering
  • Off-stage rope bite, fair and cushion only add kills against Knell (40–60%)

#5

The ant beats a grab

  • Grabs never cut the rope: a grab started on the rope line is interrupted by the ant

A damaging attack cuts the rope

  • Any active damaging hitbox near the rope cuts it
  • Potato Sack's jab, ftilt and dtilt cut it when started 4–20 frames after the call; Flint's dtilt 4–24
  • Flint's jab and ftilt lose to the ant; Knell loses to it with everything tested
